SELF-AUDIT: I verified every mechanism in source rather than trusting the reviewers; six of the thirty-five findings did not survive intact and I have said so rather than ratifying the set. Specific corrections I am making against reviewers: (a) RENDER-2's harm text says a large FINITE seed 'likewise never decays' — false, mix() is a lerp and with attackAlpha 0.6 / releaseAlpha 0.85 (AppConfig.swift:143-144) a finite seed converges in one or two frames; only NaN and Inf are absorbing, which is still enough to carry the finding. (b) RENDER-2's belt-and-braces fix proposes `replace(region:)` to zero the textures — that is illegal on a `.private` MTLTexture (replaceRegion requires .shared/.managed); the correct zeroing idioms are a blit copy from a zeroed MTLBuffer or a one-shot render pass with loadAction .clear, and the historyValid uniform should be the primary fix regardless. (c) RENDER-8's sub-claim that the existing test exercises only the equal-stride fast path is probably wrong — the source is created with nil attributes (no IOSurface) while the copy is created WITH kCVPixelBufferIOSurfacePropertiesKey, so their bytesPerRow almost certainly differ and the per-row path does run; the finding's real content (the planar branch that carries capturedImage has zero coverage) is untouched by this. (d) RENDER-5's cost arithmetic assumes 1920x1440 survives the picker, but pickVideoFormat prefers SMALLEST area at the winning fps (GeoARSessionManager.swift:698-701), so on any device that also exposes 1280x720 at the same fps the copy is ~1.38 MB, not 4.15 MB; and an IOSurface-backed CVPixelBufferCreate is nearer 100-300 us than the claimed 0.3-1.0 ms. The defect (unpooled per-call allocation + full-resolution memcpy synchronously on the main actor for pixels Vision immediately scales to ~518x518) stands; the number does not. (e) RENDER-1's proposed remedy ordering is right and I am NOT endorsing a reflexive move to install-once: ADR-035's measurement is a real, documented heat cost of attaching a postProcess callback (RealityKit switches to an offscreen render + full-screen copy the moment the slot is non-nil), and the reviewer's own note that the measurement predates the shader early-out is a reason to re-measure, not a reason to assume it is stale. (f) ARPCFG-5 partially re-litigates ADR-034's explicit written decision ('target-fps formats only, smallest resolution >= 720p, else keep ARKit's default') without new device evidence that any iOS-17-capable handset actually lacks an in-band format at or below 30 fps; I have marked it PARTIAL and asked for the instrumentation to answer that before the policy changes. What I could NOT verify and am flagging as open: I cannot run on device, so every timing number in this review (pipeline compile duration, copy cost, .utility hop latency, TSan outcome for the renderCallbacks store) is an argument from the platform contract, not a measurement — the RENDER-1 TSan harness and the RENDER-5 XCTClockMetric budget are the two that must actually be run before their fixes are sized. I also could not verify ARCore's documented lifetime contract for GARStreetscapeGeometry.mesh vertex buffers read off the main thread (GeospatialEngine.swift:443-451 retains the geometry and asserts this is sufficient); retaining the owning object is the correct-by-construction reading and I found no counter-evidence, but it is an assumption the build makes and no test pins.
